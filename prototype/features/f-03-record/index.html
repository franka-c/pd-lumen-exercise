Interaction rules

  • Record: consultations newest first, grouped by year, the first 20, then "Prikaži starije" (20 more).
  • Row: date, type, vet and clinic, the note's first line with an ellipsis, document count.
  • Consultation: the full note, the documents (PDF or image, name, date). No note: "Bilješke za ovaj pregled nisu dostupne u aplikaciji. Za pitanja se javite klinici." No documents: the section is left out.
  • Document: opens in an in-app viewer with "Podijeli" (system share sheet) and "Preuzmi". Failure: "Dokument nije moguće otvoriti. Pokušajte ponovno."
  • No push for a newly visible note. Results are announced by reception's message (F-13).
  • Deceased pet: read only (D-031). VetDesk not answering: last saved data with a notice, as F-02.
