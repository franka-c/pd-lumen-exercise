Interaction rules

  • Welcome: "Prijava" and "Nemam pozivnicu".
  • The identifier field takes an email or a phone number. Anything else: "Upišite e-mail ili broj telefona" on submit.
  • Password rule shown while typing, not after an error. "Prikaži" toggles visibility.
  • Submit buttons are disabled until their fields are filled and valid; "Šaljem…" while sending; a network failure keeps the input and offers "Pokušaj ponovno".
  • Account: name, email, phone read only, "Za promjenu podataka javite se svojoj klinici". "Obavijesti" shows "Uskoro" (F-11 is Future in 06). "Odjava" asks first.
  • The bottom bar has the four areas (D-010). Only Račun is part of F-01.
