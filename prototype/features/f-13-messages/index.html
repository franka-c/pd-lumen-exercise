Interaction rules

  • Reception: "Nova poruka" searches owner name, pet name or phone (two characters or more). F-10's detail has "Pošalji poruku vlasniku" with owner and pet chosen.
  • A template fills the text; reception can edit it. Changing the pet refills the template.
  • Before sending, the channel shows: app with push; "Šalje se e-mailom" with no read status; or "Vlasnik nema aplikaciju ni e-mail. Nazovite …" with Pošalji disabled.
  • Sent messages cannot be edited or deleted. The owner cannot reply (D-012).
  • Owner: "Od vaše klinike" on Home, newest first, the last 3 with an unread dot, then "Prikaži sve". Opened: clinic, date, full text, the link, and "Na ovu poruku ne možete odgovoriti…" with the clinic's phone. A push opens the message (D-018). No messages: the section is not shown.
