Interaction rules

  • Only a Confirmed appointment without a pending change offers "Promijeni ili otkaži", which opens a sheet: Promijeni termin, Otkaži termin, Natrag.
  • From 15 minutes before the start the button is replaced by "Za promjene nazovite kliniku" and the clinic's phone (D-028).
  • Move: only day and time change; pet, clinic, vet and type stay. The appointment's own time, and times inside the cut-off, are not offered. Four weeks of free times (D-027).
  • Send is disabled until a day and time are chosen; "Šaljem…" while sending. Success: back to Termini, the card keeps the old time and shows "Premještanje na …, čeka potvrdu klinike", toast "Zahtjev za promjenu je poslan".
  • While a move is pending the card offers only "Odustani od promjene", which removes the request at once, keeps the old time, toast "Promjena je povučena".
  • Move confirmed: the card shows the new time, push "Termin je premješten". Rejected: the old time stays, push "Promjena termina nije prihvaćena" with the reason.
  • Cancel: confirmation sheet "Otkazati termin?". Inside 24 hours it also shows the policy text (IA). Moves never show it.
  • After sending, the card shows "Otkazivanje zatraženo, čeka potvrdu klinike" and offers nothing. Confirmed: the card disappears, push "Termin je otkazan". Rejected: the appointment stands, push "Otkazivanje nije prihvaćeno" with the reason.
  • Time taken and send failure behave as in F-05. Closing the move after a time is chosen asks "Odbaciti promjenu?".
