Interaction rules

  • List: living pets by name, then "Preminuli". Card: photo or first letter, name, species and breed, age and clinic. A deceased pet shows the years.
  • Pet page: details, "Podatke vodi klinika. Za ispravak se javite klinici.", "Rezerviraj termin" (opens F-05), and Karton, Cijepljenja, Recepti (F-03, F-04, F-08).
  • Photo: "Dodaj fotografiju" from camera or gallery; then "Promijeni" and "Ukloni" (asks first). A failed save: "Fotografija nije spremljena. Pokušajte ponovno."
  • Deceased (D-031): no booking, no reminders, no prescriptions, no notifications; Karton read only; the photo stays and cannot be changed.
  • VetDesk not answering: the last saved data with "Podaci od 10:02. Osvježavanje nije uspjelo." and retry (07: VetDesk data cached for display). With nothing saved: an error with retry.
  • A pet VetDesk moves off this owner simply disappears.
