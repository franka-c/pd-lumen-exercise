Reasons

  • Appointment: vet_unavailable, call_first, other. Cancellation: late_cancellation, other. Vet, prescription: needs_checkup, other. All UNCONFIRMED placeholders (Q-010).
  • Reception, prescription, administrative only: owner_not_registered, pet_not_on_file. From the IA.

Interaction rules

  • Two roles, each with its own sign-in (D-029). Reception: Zahtjevi za termine, Zahtjevi za recepte, Poruke. Vet: Zahtjevi za recepte only.
  • Each tab shows its count of active requests; the browser tab title shows the total, for example "(4) Lumen recepcija". No sound.
  • Appointment requests: one list, the one expiring first on top. Each row: kind, pet and type, owner, time (a move shows old → new), vet, minutes left.
  • A row opens the detail in a panel on the right; below 1024 px it opens over the list.
  • "Odobri" confirms at once and the owner gets a push (D-029). "Odbij" needs a reason; free text is optional; not undoable; the owner gets a push.
  • After 15 minutes the row turns grey, "Potvrđeno automatski", with "Ukloni s liste". "Ukloni sve automatski potvrđene" clears them all.
  • If VetDesk confirmed while reception was deciding, sending shows "Ovaj zahtjev je već obrađen" and the buttons disable.
  • Prescriptions, vet: "Preuzimanje od" (default today, not in the past), then Odobri, or Odbij with a reason.
  • Prescriptions, reception: no Odobri; only "Odbij (administrativno)" with the two IA reasons.
