While a booking waits for reception, the appointment card shows "Requested,
waiting for the clinic", with the date, time and pet. The owner can cancel the
request but cannot change it.

If reception rejects the request, the owner gets a push notification and the
card changes to "Not confirmed", with the reason reception picked from a short
list. The card offers "Pick another time", which opens the booking at the same
clinic with the same pet already selected.

If reception has not answered in 24 hours, the request stays pending and the
card adds "The clinic will call you". We do not cancel it automatically.
