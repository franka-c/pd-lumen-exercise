# Hands-on prototypes

A working tool, not a deliverable. No client document, and the client never
operates them.

One per flow or part of the product, kept rough and unstyled so the conversation
is about the flow and not the colours. Nothing here is ever promoted into
`prototype/`. What a walkthrough settles goes into `log/decisions.md` and gets
rebuilt properly.

Record every correction the client makes. That correction has to reach the IA,
the prioritization and the final prototype.
