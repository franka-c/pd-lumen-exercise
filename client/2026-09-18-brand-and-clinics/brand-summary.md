# Lumen brand guidelines, summarised

Full PDF: Drive, Lumen / Brand / lumen-brand-2025.pdf

- Primary colour Lumen Teal (#0F7C7A), secondary Warm Sand (#F2E9DC), accent Coral
  (#F26B5B) for calls to action only.
- Typeface Source Sans 3 for everything. Headings weight 600, body 400.
- Rounded corners, 8 px. Photography of real pets and real staff, never stock.
- Tone: calm, plain, no exclamation marks. "Your pet", never "the patient".
- Logo: the paw mark alone is allowed at small sizes.

No components, no spacing scale, no iconography. The guidelines cover print and the
website, not an app.
