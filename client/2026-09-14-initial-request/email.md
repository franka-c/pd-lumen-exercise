**Subject:** Our own app for pet owners

Hello,

We spoke at the Zagreb clinic owners' breakfast last month. I promised to write.

Lumen runs 14 clinics, nine in Croatia and five in Slovenia. Around 60,000 active
pets on the books. Owners book by phone, which ties up reception for most of the
morning, or through the booking widget our practice software VetDesk gives us, which
is slow, ugly and does not know which vet the pet usually sees. Our clinics turn it
off more often than they leave it on.

We want our own app. Owners should be able to book and change appointments, get
reminders when a vaccination or check-up is due, see their pet's record, and request
repeat prescriptions for chronic conditions without calling. Reception needs a simple
web page to approve or reject what comes in.

We have brand guidelines from the rebrand last year and nothing else. No design
system, no app, no API documentation that I know of. Tomislav, who looks after our IT
one day a week, knows VetDesk best.

We would like to start in September and have something developers can build from by
the end of November.
