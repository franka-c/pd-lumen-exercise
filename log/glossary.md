# Glossary

The client's own vocabulary, in their words. Deliverables and the prototype use
these terms, not our synonyms for them. Where we deliberately renamed something,
say so and cite the decision.

| Their term | What it means | Our term, if different | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Term | Meaning at Lumen | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | The person responsible for a pet, who books and pays | Never "customer" or "user" in client-facing text |
| Record | The pet's clinical history as held in VetDesk | Petra: "the record", never "the file" |
| Repeat prescription | A prescription for a chronic condition, renewed without a new consultation | Always vet-approved, D-004 |
| Reception | The front desk staff at a clinic, who manage requests | They use "reception", not "admin" |
| VetDesk | The practice management software all 14 clinics run on | Partner API status unknown, Q-001 |
| Usual vet | The vet a pet has seen most, as VetDesk records it | D-002 |
| Reminder | An item VetDesk returns as due for a pet, with a type, a due date and a clinic | D-014 |
| From your clinic | The list of reception messages on Home | D-018 |
