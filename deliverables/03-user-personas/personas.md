# User personas

Fictional client, created for an internal DECODE exercise.

Three personas from the kickoff and the four week-two interviews (two owners, two
receptionists). Farm clients are excluded (D-001).

## Ivana, the family owner

Thirty-eight, two children, a six-year-old labrador and a cat that arrived with the
children. Books from her phone during the commute. Wants the vet the dog knows
(D-002), the next free Saturday slot, and to stop being the family member who
remembers the vaccination dates. Reads push notifications, ignores email until the
weekend. Frustration today: calling at 07:30 and holding.

Jobs. Book and move a check-up without calling. Know what is due. Show the vet's
notes to her partner.

## Branko, the owner of an old dog on medication

Sixty-seven, retired, a twelve-year-old dog with epilepsy. Calls the clinic once a
month for the repeat prescription and chats with reception while he waits. Will
keep calling. Uses WhatsApp and email on an Android phone his daughter set up. Would
use the app for one thing: requesting the prescription so he does not have to time
his call to when a vet is free. Needs to see when it was approved and when he can
collect (D-004).

Jobs. Request the repeat prescription. See its status. Get the reminder by email.

## Sanja, reception at Lumen Trešnjevka

Twenty-nine, three years on the desk, the one everyone asks. Two screens, VetDesk on
one, the phone queue on the other. Will not install anything (D-005). Wants a multiple
list of requests she can clear between calls, approve in two clicks, and reject with
a reason the owner actually reads. Worried the app will double her work if it does
not write to VetDesk.

Jobs. Clear the request list. Reject with a reason. Message an owner about results
(not in scope, noted).
